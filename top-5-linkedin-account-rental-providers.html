Top 5 Providers to Rent LinkedIn Accounts and Profiles in 2026

Comparing rental services? Here are five providers to consider, what they advertise, how their prices differ, and the questions worth asking before you pay.

RARentAccounts EditorialNovember 13, 20269-minute read
The short version

LinkedOutreach, LinkedAccount, LinkedRent.io, MirrorProfiles and LinkUnity all market access to prepared LinkedIn profiles for business outreach. Their offers differ in account history, connection counts, setup support, optional subscriptions and how they handle account problems. This is an editorial shortlist based on published information, not a hands-on product test.

Renting a LinkedIn account usually means paying a service provider to access and use a prepared profile for prospecting. Some services bundle profile setup or technical support. Others emphasize regional availability, verified identities, or lower per-account rates when you rent several profiles.

Those differences matter because the cheapest advertised plan can cost more once you add verification, Sales Navigator, or separate software. Equally important, LinkedIn's User Agreement prohibits members from sharing or transferring their accounts. Rental providers cannot eliminate that platform-policy risk, regardless of the age or verification status of a profile.

How to choose a provider

There is no single offer that suits every team. Start with the characteristics of the profile your campaign needs, then compare the complete monthly cost and what happens after delivery.

Six questions worth asking before payment

  1. Identity: Who owns the account, and can that person verify it when prompted?
  2. Region: Can the provider show the actual countries and languages available?
  3. Access: Will you receive a login, a managed session or a service where the owner sends on your behalf?
  4. Software: Is your existing outreach tool compatible, and are any subscriptions included?
  5. Support: How long does setup take, what happens after a restriction, and when is replacement excluded?
  6. Contract: What is the full monthly charge, renewal date, cancellation deadline and refund policy?

Use the same checklist for every vendor. A 500-connection profile and a 100-connection profile can serve different campaigns, but neither figure proves reply performance. Similarly, a verified badge does not guarantee continued access.

Frequently asked questions

Is renting a LinkedIn account allowed by LinkedIn?

LinkedIn's User Agreement says members must not share or transfer accounts. That creates a platform-policy risk with profile rental arrangements. Check the current agreement and the specific service model before purchasing.

How much does it cost to rent a LinkedIn profile?

For the five services listed here, published or directory-reported base rates start around $129–$135 per account per month for several providers. MirrorProfiles publishes separate euro and dollar rates by geography. Add-ons, volume tiers and availability change the total.

Does a rented LinkedIn account come with Sales Navigator?

Do not assume it does. LinkedOutreach lists Sales Navigator as a separate $50/month add-on, and MirrorProfiles says it is not included by default. Ask the other providers for the exact subscription included in your quote.

Are aged or ID-verified LinkedIn profiles guaranteed to avoid restrictions?

No. The account's age, network and verification status cannot remove policy risk or guarantee access. Read the provider's written recovery, replacement and refund conditions.

What is the difference between buying and renting an account?

Buying is usually described as a one-time transfer, whereas renting involves recurring payment for access and possibly setup or support. Both models require careful consideration of ownership, authorization and LinkedIn's account-sharing restrictions.
