How We Compare Providers

The key checks before committing to a rental plan.

01 / Profile access

Who controls the account?

Ask about identity, login arrangements, and whether the account holder is involved in verification or recovery.

02 / Contract terms

What do you actually pay?

Compare base rent, optional verification, additional tools, geographic availability and cancellation conditions.

03 / Support

What happens after a restriction?

Read the written replacement and refund policy rather than relying on marketing guarantees.

04 / Evidence

Can the claims be verified?

Separate details published by the vendor from independent observations. We have not run a hands-on test of these five services.

Platform-policy note. LinkedIn's User Agreement restricts sharing or transferring member accounts. Account age, verification or supplier support cannot remove that risk.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about rental providers.

What is the cheapest provider on this list?

The lowest reported starting figure in this comparison is $129 per account monthly, but some of those values come from third-party listings. Additional features and different currencies affect the total.

Can I get ID-verified LinkedIn profiles?

Some providers advertise identity-verified profiles or offer verification as an add-on. Confirm the specific account's status and what the service means by verification.

Are LinkedIn account rentals safe?

Profile rentals can violate LinkedIn's account-sharing rules and carry access and restriction risks. No supplier can guarantee an account stays available.

How were these providers selected?

The five services follow the requested editorial shortlist. We compared publicly available details, not measured reply rates or operational results.
