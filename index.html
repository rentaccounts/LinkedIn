EDITORIAL METHOD

What we look at

A repeatable checklist for writing and reading product comparisons.

01 / ACCESS

Who owns the profile?

We distinguish personal profile rental, managed services, and using your own account. Their risk and control models differ.

02 / EVIDENCE

Can the claim be checked?

We separate published vendor claims, official policies, and independently verified observations.

03 / COST

What are you paying for?

We look beyond the headline monthly price at access, replacement terms, service levels, and cancellation conditions.

04 / RISK

What can go wrong?

Restrictions, lost access, security exposure, and a dependency on third-party accounts belong in the decision.

QUESTIONS TO ASK

Before you commit

Are rented LinkedIn accounts permitted by LinkedIn?+

LinkedIn's User Agreement says members must not share or transfer their accounts, and it prohibits accessing another person's account. Read the current User Agreement before considering a provider.

Does a dedicated account eliminate restriction risk?+

No service can guarantee that a LinkedIn account will never be restricted. Ask vendors for clear support and recovery policies rather than promises of zero risk.

Do you publish provider rankings from testing?+

Only when the test method, period, and supporting evidence can be described. At launch, this site publishes evaluation guides rather than unsupported performance rankings.
