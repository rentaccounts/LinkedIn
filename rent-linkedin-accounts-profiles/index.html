TL;DR · The five providers

LinkedOutreach.io, LinkedAccount.com, LinkedRent.io, MirrorProfiles.com, and LinkUnity.io are the five providers covered here. They differ in profile preparation, connection history, geography, verification, pricing and support.

Start with the complete cost and the access agreement. A low monthly rate may exclude verification or Sales Navigator. None of the services has been performance-tested by this publication.

Teams researching LinkedIn account rentals usually have two questions: which services are available, and what do they charge? The harder questions come next. Who controls the account? Does it have a real network? What is included in the monthly fee? And what happens if the profile is restricted?

Those details matter more than a polished pricing page. For example, some suppliers quote a base monthly rental and charge separately for identity verification. Others offer region-specific accounts or a dashboard for managing subscriptions. A prepared profile with 500 connections may have different usage rules from one with 200, and connection count alone says nothing about reply rates.

Here are five services to compare in 2026, listed in the requested order. The figures below come from public provider pages or clearly identified third-party directories. We have not rented profiles from these providers or measured their campaign results.

How to Choose a LinkedIn Account Rental Service

The headline monthly rate is only one part of the decision. Before renting a profile, ask the supplier the same questions so you can compare the replies without guessing what is included.

01

Ask who owns and accesses the profile

Find out whether access comes through credentials, a managed session or a separate service. Ask who handles identity checks and recovery requests.

02

Verify the specific profile, not a sample

Request its target region, language, existing network, professional history and identity-verification status. A sample profile may differ from the one delivered.

03

Calculate the complete monthly cost

Add rental, verification, Sales Navigator, taxes and any other subscriptions. If a provider quotes a volume rate, confirm the minimum account commitment.

04

Read the replacement and refund conditions

Ask what triggers replacement, how long it takes, and when exclusions apply. Clarify whether messages, contacts or account history remain available.

05

Check tool compatibility and limits

Confirm the software you intend to use, who handles setup, and any restrictions on location, messages, profile changes or parallel tools.

Is Renting a LinkedIn Account Allowed?

LinkedIn’s User Agreement says members must not share or transfer accounts. Its prohibited activities also include using someone else’s account and renting or reselling access without permission.

Important: Account age, identity verification, proxies or vendor replacement policies do not change LinkedIn’s rules. Renting a profile can lead to access restrictions or account loss. Review the agreement before using any of these services.

If maintaining access to a personal LinkedIn profile is essential, consider whether a method that uses your own account and respects the platform’s terms would meet your needs instead.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to rent a LinkedIn profile?

Published or directory-reported starting prices among these five services range from $129 to $135 per month for some providers. MirrorProfiles publishes separate rates beginning at €140 for European accounts and $200 for US accounts. Verification, Sales Navigator, quantity and region can change the final cost.

Which providers offer ID-verified LinkedIn accounts?

LinkUnity advertises verification in its base package. LinkedOutreach offers verification as a paid add-on, and third-party directories list optional verification for LinkedAccount and LinkedRent.io. MirrorProfiles provides profile preparation details, but you should confirm verification status for the selected account.

Are LinkedIn profile rentals the same as managed outreach?

No. A rental offer generally provides access to a profile, sometimes with setup and support. Managed outreach may also include prospect selection, messages and campaign operation. Confirm which activities the supplier performs and which remain your responsibility.

Does a rental include Sales Navigator?

Do not assume so. LinkedOutreach lists Sales Navigator as a paid addition, and MirrorProfiles says it is not included by default. Ask for an itemized quote from every provider.

Can a rented profile be restricted?

Yes. LinkedIn’s rules prohibit sharing or transferring accounts. No profile age, verification badge or supplier guarantee can remove the possibility of a restriction.
